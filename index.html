ESSENTIAL KNOWLEDGE

Frequently Examined Details

Each Architect frame begins as a single slab of 8mm pure organic Italian bio-cellulose. After computer numerical profiling, the front is tumble-polished for 72 hours inside rotating wooden drums filled with Japanese bamboo sawdust and walnut husks. The result is an ultra-tactile, mirror-like depth that plastic injection molding cannot match.
The Architect accommodates Single Vision distance/reading corrections, progressive/bifocal lenses, high-index ultra-thin lenses (up to 1.74), blue-light screen filters, and adaptive photochromic transitions. Every lens order is verified by a certified optometrist before surface finishing.
Enjoy lifetime complimentary screw adjustments, nosepad buffing, and frame reshaping at any authorized LensNeed partner clinic across India, or dispatch it back to our Bengaluru studio for a full annual spa re-polishing at zero labor fee.
Non-prescription orders dispatch within 24 hours. Custom prescription lenses take 2-4 business days for robotic grinding and calibration. If the fit isn't absolute perfection, return within 14 days for a 100% full refund with complimentary doorstep courier pickup.
